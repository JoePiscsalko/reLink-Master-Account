Data gaps and how the master file was assembled

Two role vocabularies, deliberately kept apart

Acquisition coordinator and sell-to coordinator are internal reLink owners, from the account hierarchy export. Authorized redeemer and 360 disposition champion are customer-side contacts, from the contact roles export — the champion is stored in Salesforce as 360 Program Champion, mapped here to the disposition champion column. Contacts flagged “no longer there” were dropped. Four contact accounts — CME, Unity Health, BioMedovation and VHC Health — did not match an account in the hierarchy and carry no roll-up.

Open TPS is linked for 360 Current only

Three TPS exports have been merged — 1,367 YTD records, 87 of them open. Account names were supplied for the 360 Current slice, so all 39 of those open records now nest under their child account and roll up to 29 health systems. The other 48 open records — 36 transactional, 6 360 target, 6 unclassified — still carry only Queue TPS Seller Account Status, a status value rather than a join key. Adding the Seller Account lookup to that export closes the remainder.

Escrow covers 360 partners only

The escrow report is filtered to reLink 360 Partner = True, so 1,319 accounts carry GL detail. The 5,511 transactional accounts under those same parents are all present in the hierarchy but show no escrow line — that is a filter boundary, not missing money.

2026 annual budget is a tracker figure, not a Salesforce field

Budgets come from the 2026 Annual Budget column of the Master Tracker workbook, read across its all-accounts, net-new and strategic sheets — 406 rows collapsing to 168 distinct accounts worth $19.5M. The same account is often spelled differently sheet to sheet (Jefferson Health / Jefferson; Lurie Childrens / Laurie Childrens), so rows are deduplicated on a normalized key and the highest figure taken, never summed. 154 accounts matched a health system in the hierarchy, totalling $18.2M; the remaining 14 are net-new prospects with no Salesforce account yet, worth $1.31M — among them Premier, MultiCare, McClaren, Maimonides, Dayton Childrens and Lurie Childrens. Where a parent legitimately carries several distinct tracker lines they are summed: GE HealthCare rolls up five, Kaiser Permanente two regions.

What was excluded, and what reconciles

Every account that rolls up to an ultimate parent is included — 360 current, 360 target and transactional alike. Dropped from the master: eBay, Shopify, Proxibid and auction-channel buyer records (sales channels, not health systems, carrying no escrow), and roughly 55,000 one-off individual buyer accounts with no parent and no escrow. Portal balance ties to the source report exactly at $3,715,618.

Join logic

Accounts are keyed on Salesforce account name. All 1,319 escrow child accounts matched the hierarchy export exactly — no fuzzy matching was needed. Where the escrow report assigns an ultimate parent and the hierarchy export leaves it blank, the escrow parent wins. Contract-gap names were matched on a normalized form that strips parenthetical abbreviations.
